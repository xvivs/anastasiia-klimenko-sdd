What ties it together

Four patterns run through every chapter, and they are what a Lead PM or General Manager role needs.

  • I build systems, not one-off wins.

    The omnichannel stack, the LiveOps engine and the test factory all kept working after I moved on.

  • I think in business, not features.

    Coming from product marketing, I start from revenue, retention and unit economics, and I follow the money even outside my own area.

  • I find the real constraint.

    The insufficient-funds insight is the clearest example: I changed the question instead of pushing harder on the wrong lever.

  • I grow with scope.

    Each step added responsibility: from content, to a whole marketing function, to leading PMs and a content team, to a 0→1 business in a new market.
